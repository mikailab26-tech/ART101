Welome to the show!

Every place has a story, some of which are best left untold. Behind every door lie buried secrets, energetic spirits and horrors that refuse to stay in the past. Step into this world of madness, sanity is an illusion and the line between death and living is never quite clear. Every stranger has a secret, every nightmare has a beginning, and every story has a price. Are you willing to pay the price? If so, welcome to the show, where the question is not whether you believe in monsters, but whether you will recognize one when it standing right in front of you.
